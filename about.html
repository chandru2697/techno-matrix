About Techno Matrix

Driven by Precision. Built on Engineering Excellence.

Your Trusted Partner in Precision Manufacturing

Founded in 2017 by Mr. N.V. Kannan, Techno Matrix is a precision engineering and manufacturing company based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. Backed by over 20 years of professional experience gained across leading aerospace industries in Singapore, we bring extensive technical knowledge and manufacturing expertise to every project.

We specialize in precision CNC machining and advanced engineering solutions, serving diverse industrial sectors with a strong commitment to quality, accuracy and reliability.

With continuously expanding infrastructure, next-generation machinery and a highly skilled workforce, we strive to meet evolving industry demands while delivering consistent value to our customers.

Our Journey

A Journey of Growth, Innovation & Excellence

Since our establishment in 2017, Techno Matrix has continuously strengthened its manufacturing capabilities through strategic investments in advanced machinery, infrastructure and technical expertise. Every milestone reflects our commitment to precision, quality and continuous growth.

2017

The Beginning

Started operations with two VMC machines and established an ISO-certified manufacturing setup.

2018

Capacity Expansion

Expanded production capacity by adding four additional VMC machines.

2019

Entering New Industries

Diversified operations and entered the high-precision Oil & Gas sector.

2021

Infrastructure Development

Relocated operations to our own 5,500 sq. ft. manufacturing facility.

2022

Advanced Technology Integration

Added a Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM), three Turning Centres, one 5-Axis machine and one 5-Axis Sliding Head machine with bar feeder.

2023

Facility Expansion

Added a new 6,000 sq. ft. building and secured an additional 6,000 sq. ft. of vacant land for future expansion.

2024

Aerospace Development

Added four VMCs, one 5-Axis machine and one Wire EDM machine. Initiated our development programme to officially serve the Aerospace industry.

2025

Strengthening Production

Expanded production capacity with five VMCs, two Turning Centres, one Laser Marking machine and one Tapping machine.

2026

Advancing Manufacturing Excellence

Added three VMCs, one Turn-Mill Centre and one Vision Measuring System (VMS). Successfully achieved AS9100D / EN 9100:2018 certification.
